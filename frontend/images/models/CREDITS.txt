3D Vehicle Models
=================

The .glb vehicle models in this folder are from the Kenney "Car Kit"
(https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit), released under the Creative Commons Zero
(CC0 1.0 Universal / Public Domain) license.
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Created / distributed by Kenney (www.kenney.nl).
No attribution is required by the license, but credit is given here voluntarily.

File mapping (renamed for this app):
  sedan.glb      <- Car Kit "sedan"
  suv.glb        <- Car Kit "suv"
  hatchback.glb  <- Car Kit "hatchback-sports"
  bike.glb       <- Car Kit "motorcycle"
  truck.glb      <- Car Kit "truck"
  ev.glb         <- Car Kit "sedan-sports"
